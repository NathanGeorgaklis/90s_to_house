Tempo & grid

Tempo and beat position are estimated from the audio. If the drums flam against the song, use Nudge; if the chords land on the wrong beat, use Move bar start; if the speed-up sounds chipmunky, try ×2 to slow the song down instead.

Key

The bassline and chords follow the groove key. If you know the song's key, set it and the groove key follows the pitch change from re-tempo. Otherwise set it by ear, or turn Bass and Chords down.
